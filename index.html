Statement

My career began in Detroit, where I built college access programs from the ground up for Title I students who had every reason to doubt that higher education was meant for them. When you build the right systems, develop the right leaders, and refuse to accept anything less than excellence, students exceed every expectation placed on them.

From those founding years, I moved into federal policy, spending nearly a decade at the U.S. Department of Education shaping the systems that govern educational access for millions of students — then into education law in Baltimore, and accreditation oversight at the national level. Each chapter sharpened the same conviction: that the institutions, leaders, and systems above a student are responsible for making an excellent education real.

I am a practicing attorney, higher education professor, and the founder of Pingree Education, a college counseling venture committed to expanding access for first-generation and low-income students in the DMV area.
